VOCABULARY

affirmer que (ah-feer-MAY kuh) - to claim that
prétendre que (pray-TAHN-druh kuh) - to claim (with doubt)
estimer que (ehs-tee-MAY kuh) - to believe, to consider that
souligner que (soo-lee-NYAY kuh) - to stress that
mettre en avant (MEH-truh ahn nah-VAHN) - to put forward, to highlight

la preuve (lah PRUHV) - evidence, proof

GRAMMAR
Reporting verbs - Verbs that introduce someone's opinion, in increasing strength of commitment: estimer que, penser que, soutenir que, affirmer que, assurer que, prétendre que. They take the indicative or the conditional: l'auteur estime que c'est la seule solution. Other reporting verbs: souligner que, rappeler que, ajouter que, reconnaître que, dénoncer le fait que.
The journalistic conditional - Journalists use the conditional to report information that isn't confirmed: la mairie aurait reçu plusieurs plaintes, le ministre serait en voyage, il y aurait trois blessés. It tells the reader to be careful. Do not translate it as a real conditional: it marks distance and uncertainty, not a hypothesis.
