VOCABULARY
le bilan (luh bee-LAHN) - review, assessment
nuancer son avis (new-ahn-SAY sohn ah-VEE) - to qualify one's opinion
argumenter (ahr-gew-mahn-TAY) - to argue (build a case)
l'aisance (leh-ZAHNSS) - ease, fluency
exigeant / exigeante (ehk-see-ZHAHN) - demanding
un article de fond (uhn nar-TEE-kluh duh FOHN) - in-depth article
il reste du chemin (eel REHST dew shuh-MAN) - there's still a way to go
le niveau C1 (luh nee-VOH say-OON) - level C1 (advanced)
bonne continuation (bohn kohn-tee-new-ah-SYOHN) - all the best

GRAMMAR
What you can now do at B2 - You can: use the past subjunctive and the subjunctive after emotions, doubt and concessions; use compound relative pronouns and ce à quoi / ce dont; nominalise and write formal texts; use participles and the causative faire plus infinitive; present arguments with connectors; recognise registers and literary tenses; use idioms and litotes.
Looking ahead to C1 - At C1, aim for greater precision, idiomatic range and stylistic control: more nominalisation, more subtle concessive and hypothetical structures, literary and journalistic texts, formal and spoken registers in full. Keep practising with real material: articles, debates, podcasts, novels. Thank and congratulate: félicitations, bonne continuation, merci pour tout.
