GRAMMAR
Comme si + imparfait - Comme si introduces an unreal comparison and takes the imparfait or the plus-que-parfait: la brume enveloppait les toits comme si elle voulait les protéger; il se comportait comme si rien ne s'était passé. It never takes the conditional or the present subjunctive. It's common in literary description.
Tenses in literary texts - Descriptions and background use the imparfait. Main actions use the passé simple in literary prose. The plus-que-parfait gives earlier events. To analyse a text: identify the narrator, the point of view, the figures of speech, and the lexical fields.
