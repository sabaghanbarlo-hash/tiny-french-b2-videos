VOCABULARY
le registre de langue (luh ruh-ZHEES-truh duh LAHNG) - language register
soutenu / soutenue (soo-tuh-NEW) - formal, elevated
courant / courante (koo-RAHN) - standard, everyday
familier / familière (fah-mee-LYAY) - informal, colloquial
l'interlocuteur (lan-tehr-loh-kew-TUHR) - the person one is speaking to
le véhicule / la bagnole (luh vay-ee-KEWL) - vehicle / car (slang)
l'inversion (lan-vehr-SYOHN) - inversion
cela / ça (suh-LAH / SAH) - that (formal / informal)
adapter le ton (ah-dahp-TAY luh TOHN) - to adapt the tone

GRAMMAR
Three registers - Familier: dropped ne, tu / on, slang, tag questions, ça. Courant: complete negatives, standard vocabulary, est-ce que questions. Soutenu: inversion, precise vocabulary, cela, passé simple in writing, subjunctive in more contexts. Choose the register according to the reader, the situation and the medium.
Formal transformations - To raise the register: use inversion in questions, keep ne, replace ça by cela, replace familiar nouns with precise ones, use nominalisation, and avoid contractions and fillers. To lower it: drop ne, use on for nous, and use tu with friends.
