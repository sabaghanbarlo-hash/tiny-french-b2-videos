[grammar]
grammar: Comme si + imparfait | Comme si introduces an unreal comparison and takes the imparfait or the plus-que-parfait: la brume enveloppait les toits comme si elle voulait les protéger; il se comportait comme si rien ne s'était passé. It never takes the conditional or the present subjunctive. It's common in literary description.
example: Comme si elle voulait les protéger. | As if she wanted to protect them.
example: Comme si rien ne s'était passé. | As if nothing had happened.
grammar: Tenses in literary texts | Descriptions and background use the imparfait. Main actions use the passé simple in literary prose. The plus-que-parfait gives earlier events. To analyse a text: identify the narrator, the point of view, the figures of speech, and the lexical fields.
example: La ville dormait encore. | The town was still asleep.
example: Il ouvrit la fenêtre et respira. | He opened the window and breathed.
