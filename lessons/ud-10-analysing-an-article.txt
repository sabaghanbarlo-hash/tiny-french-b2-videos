[grammar]
grammar: Reporting verbs | Verbs that introduce someone's opinion, in increasing strength of commitment: estimer que, penser que, soutenir que, affirmer que, assurer que, prétendre que. They take the indicative or the conditional: l'auteur estime que c'est la seule solution. Other reporting verbs: souligner que, rappeler que, ajouter que, reconnaître que, dénoncer le fait que.
example: Il estime que c'est la seule solution. | He believes it's the only solution.
example: Il prétend que les commerces n'en souffriraient pas. | He claims shops wouldn't suffer.
grammar: The journalistic conditional | Journalists use the conditional to report information that isn't confirmed: la mairie aurait reçu plusieurs plaintes, le ministre serait en voyage, il y aurait trois blessés. It tells the reader to be careful. Do not translate it as a real conditional: it marks distance and uncertainty, not a hypothesis.
example: La mairie aurait reçu plusieurs plaintes. | The town hall has reportedly received several complaints.
example: Il y aurait trois blessés. | There are said to be three injured.
